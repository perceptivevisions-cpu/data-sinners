They took his water. He took a pitchfork.

On a computer

W A S D to walk, Shift to run. Arrow keys to look (or the trackpad). F or click to jab. Space to dodge. R for Pitchfork Fury. Q to swap to the manure cannon. E or right-click for the hay-hook. Esc to pause.

On a phone

Left thumb to walk. Drag on the right to look. Tap to jab. Buttons for Dodge, Hook, Fury, and the manure cannon.

Level 1 · Pump Station Outpost
  1. Run the Cogitorium man off your barn. Sneak up and he’s stunned.
  2. Smash the 4 intake grates. One runs hot, one is shielded, one is guarded.
  3. Wreck the server pods, or jab a pod’s red cooling valve and let it cook.
  4. Manure stuns anyone. Jab a stunned executive (flashing teal) for a glory kill and a canteen of water.
  5. The hay-hook yanks anyone to you: it grounds drones and rips off spin.
  6. Watch for Cloud Consultants (they fly), PR reps (break their spin), and lobbyists (don’t take the money).
  7. Fill Pitchfork Fury, then unleash it.
  8. Storm Pump Station No. 7, take down the Synergizer, and pull the main lever.
